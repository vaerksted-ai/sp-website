Brand basics

Synthetic Practitioner should feel like Nordic healthcare at its best: calm, clear, honest and quietly warm. Lots of white space, one confident green, plain words.

The mark

The Selbu rose: the eight-petal star knitted into Scandinavian mittens and sweaters for generations, drawn here as a healing flower. Its four long petals form a medical plus, so it reads as care at a glance. The colour version is two-tone (scrubs green with washed-scrubs diagonal petals); the white and one-colour versions use a single colour, with thin gaps keeping the petals apart.

Colour

Scrubs green carries the brand. Everything else stays quiet. Red is reserved for emergencies and warnings, never for decoration.

Typography

Albert Sans, a geometric sans-serif inspired by Scandinavian minimalism (free, Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License). SemiBold with tight tracking for headings; Regular for text.

Voice

We sound like

  • A calm, kind GP at 2 a.m.
  • Short sentences, plain Danish and English
  • Honest about limits: "a doctor takes over here"

We never

  • Pretend the AI is a human or a "læge"
  • Promise outcomes or use fear
  • Use emojis, exclamation-heavy hype or medical jargon
