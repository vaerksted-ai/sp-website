Old Norse meets AI, in the Værksted family. The seal pairs a gold rim and the name in Elder Futhark with a healing Selbu rose in aurora light. The website itself stays light and scrubs green, because a health service has to feel trustworthy first.

The seal

A sibling of the Værksted medallion. Rim: gold metal, as in the Værksted seal. Rune ring: "Synthetic Practitioner" spelled letter by letter in Elder Futhark, the way Værksted writes its own name. Centre: the Selbu rose from Scandinavian knitwear, drawn as a healing flower whose four long petals form a medical plus, lit in the green-to-blue arc of Værksted's Bifröst gradient: the northern lights. Disc: the Værksted cosmos, tinted scrubs green.

Colour

Scrubs green carries the site; gold and the cosmos come from Værksted and appear in the seal, the waitlist and social images. Aurora is for small glowing accents only. Red is reserved for emergencies.

Voice

We sound like

  • A calm, kind GP at 2 a.m.
  • Short sentences, plain Danish and English
  • Honest about limits: "a doctor takes over here"

We never

  • Pretend the AI is a human or a "læge"
  • Promise outcomes or use fear
  • Use emojis, hype or medical jargon
