synthetic practitioner

A Selbu rose, the eight-petal star of Scandinavian knitwear, charted stitch by stitch. A knitting chart is a grid of pixels, so the mark is Nordic craft and digital at once: a warm, familiar pattern made by a machine. Its four long petals join into a medical plus, and one mint stitch glows at the heart: the AI.

The mark

  • Grid: 13 × 13 stitches, each a rounded square with a small gap, like a knitted or pixel chart.
  • Plus: the four long petals, joined through the centre, in scrubs green.
  • Star: the four diagonal petals in washed scrubs, completing the Selbu rose.
  • Heart: a single mint stitch, the AI. It's the only accent colour in the mark.

Colour

Scrubs green carries everything. Mint is the AI's glow and appears in small doses: the heart stitch, the three stitches before a label. Red is for emergencies only.

Motif

The stitch grid is the one recurring motif: faint stitch dots on dark sections, three stitches leading each label. Keep it quiet. It should feel like texture, not decoration.

Voice

We sound like

  • A calm, kind GP at 2 a.m.
  • Short sentences, plain Danish and English
  • Honest about limits: "a doctor takes over here"

We never

  • Pretend the AI is a human or a "læge"
  • Promise outcomes or use fear
  • Use emojis, hype or medical jargon
